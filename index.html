Pick a layer

Grab and drag the figure ← → to open or close the right cup. Click any of its layers, or a button above, to see what it is and what it does. Five layers, from the ear outward: pad, baffle, driver, housing, grille.

Ripples: front wave out of the left pad, back wave out of the right grilleSignal: music current from the amplifier through the cableAn open-back headphone on a stand. The cable plugs into the left cup only. Take the right cup apart and its layers line up along the ear axis: velour pad, baffle with front fabric, the driver, the housing with its open spoke cage, and the perforated grille. The driver sits at a slight angle, so sound reaches the ear from slightly in front. Each diaphragm stroke sends one wave to the ear and an opposite wave backward, out through the cage and grille: that is what open-back means. Motion is slowed and enlarged.
